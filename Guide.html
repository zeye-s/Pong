Controls

Player 1

Up: W
Down: S
Ability: D


Player 2

Up: I
Down: K
Ability: L


The cooldown for abilities is 60 seconds. Make sure to select an ability before starting the game.
Just a recomendation: do not hold the keys because holding two keys at the same time will cause one of them to freeze so just click.

Abilities

Time Lord

Stops opponent movement for 5 seconds



Frost Zone

Changes the ball speed to 0.5 no matter its current speed (default speed is 1 and increases by 0.5 everytime ball is hit). Resets upon hitting the ball



Flame Frenzy

Changes the ball speed to 5. Grants 1 second invincibility(no score added if you missed the ball). Resets upon scoring



Bastion

Changes the paddle size to 250(default 100) for 10 seconds



Dai Ichi No Bakudan

Yes This is a Jojo reference
Opponent loses 2 points if they touch the ball. Resets upon touching the ball



Determination

Grants invincibility for 5 seconds
